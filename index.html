Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

BrainZap does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

This policy explains how the BrainZap puzzle app for Android (package app.brainzap.com, "the app") handles your information. It applies only to the app.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers, and does not use the internet. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID, or any other personal or sensitive data.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Your puzzle progress, stars, and saved games
  • Your statistics, streaks, and badges
  • Your settings, such as theme, sound, and vibration

This data never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it permanently.

Device backup

If you have turned on Android's built-in backup, Android may include the app's settings and progress in your personal device backup, for example to your Google account. This is handled by Android and Google under your backup settings and Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive or have access to it.

Permissions

The app requests only one permission: vibration, used for optional haptic feedback while you play. You can turn this off in the app's settings. The app does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions.

Ads, analytics, and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not sell, trade, or share any data with any third party, because we have no data to share.

Sharing your results

If you choose to tap Share result, the app opens Android's standard share sheet with a short text summary of your puzzle result. You decide whether and where to send it. We do not see or store what you share.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, chat, or in-app purchases.

Security

Because your data stays inside the app's private storage on your device and is never sent over a network, it is protected by Android's built-in app sandboxing.

Your rights

Since we do not hold any of your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete. You stay in full control of the data on your device and can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app starts handling data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
